# Lead Intelligence & Market Research Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a business lead intelligence and market research dashboard developed during a Business Analytics internship.

The dashboard analyzes local businesses in Roorkee to identify their digital presence, service requirements, priority levels, and potential areas for digital marketing and web development services.

## Objective

The main objectives of this project were:

- Analyze businesses based on their digital presence
- Identify businesses with website and social media gaps
- Understand demand for digital services
- Classify leads based on priority level
- Identify business categories with higher lead concentration
- Generate insights to support lead generation and outreach

## Dataset

The analysis was based on 175 business records covering 14 business categories in Roorkee.

The dataset included information related to:

- Business category
- Website status
- Social media status
- Google Business Profile status
- Services required
- Lead priority
- Business location

The original lead-level dataset is not included in this repository due to the presence of business contact and lead information.

## Tools Used

- Google Looker Studio
- Google Sheets

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for total records, high-priority leads, website demand, social media demand, and categories covered
- Website status analysis
- Service demand analysis
- Business category breakdown
- Google Business Profile status analysis
- Social media status analysis
- Priority level distribution
- Top high-priority business leads
- Key insights and recommendations

## Key Findings

- 175 business records were analyzed across 14 categories.
- 63 businesses were identified as high-priority leads.
- Website development was the most requested service, with 108 businesses identified.
- Social media services were required by 86 businesses.
- Restaurants, clothing businesses, and healthcare-related businesses represented major categories in the dataset.
- A considerable number of businesses had inactive or no social media presence.
- Several businesses had active social media profiles but lacked professional websites.

## Business Insights

The analysis highlighted opportunities for digital marketing and web development services among local businesses.

The dashboard was used to identify high-priority businesses and understand the types of digital services most commonly required, supporting targeted lead generation and outreach.
